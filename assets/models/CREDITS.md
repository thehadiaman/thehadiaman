# 3D models

All models in this folder are by Kenney (https://kenney.nl) and released under
Creative Commons Zero (CC0 1.0, public domain). Attribution is not required,
but credit is given here anyway.

| Folder | Kenney kit |
| --- | --- |
| characters/ | Mini Characters (2.0) |
| nature/ | Nature Kit |
| city/ | City Kit (Commercial) |

Changes made for this site: the character files (including the robot,
character-robot.glb, used as the rescue flyer, and the people in the Dubai
arrivals crowd and the zombies) had unused animation clips removed to save space (the NPCs
borrow the avatar's clips); aid-glasses.glb gets clear lenses and dark rims
at load time; and the suburban
houses' green roofs are recoloured to terracotta at load time.

Everything else in the world (terrain, sea, sky, airport terminals, control
tower, the plane, the Sabre mast, the Redis cabinet, the Dubai supertall, the
rescuer's jetpack and the split-flap boards) is built from primitives in assets/js/world/.
