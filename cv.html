Projects

WhatsApp AI Support Desk

In progress

A multi-tenant WhatsApp support desk for small businesses in India and the Gulf. A Claude Haiku assistant on AWS Bedrock answers from the business’s own FAQs, catalogue and orders, using RAG over pgvector and tool calling. It hands the conversation to staff when needed, and works in English, Hindi, Malayalam and Arabic, voice notes included.

The WhatsApp Cloud API pipeline is built to tolerate faults: signed webhooks are queued in BullMQ with duplicate suppression, per-conversation ordering, and retries with backoff into a dead-letter table. In a 1,000-message load test with injected faults, no reply was duplicated or sent out of order.

TypeScript · Next.js · NestJS · Socket.IO · PostgreSQL with row-level security · Redis · Docker · Terraform. Unit, integration, Playwright and eval suites run in CI. Tested in mock mode; not deployed yet.
